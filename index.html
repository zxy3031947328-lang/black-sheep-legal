Privacy Policy for Black Sheep

Last updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Black Sheep collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information depending on how you use the app:

  • Email address and authentication information when you register or sign in.
  • Profile information such as nickname and avatar.
  • App content and usage data you create in the app, including progress, daily records, and related growth data.
  • Purchase status and subscription entitlement information provided through Apple StoreKit.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To sync your progress and records across sessions or devices when you sign in.
  • To restore purchases and verify subscription status.
  • To provide app features such as avatar upload, cloud sync, and account deletion.

3. Services We Use

  • Firebase Authentication for account registration, login, and account management.
  • Firebase Firestore for storing synced progress, records, and related account data.
  • Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases, subscriptions, and purchase restoration.

4. Photo Access

If you choose to upload a custom avatar, the app may request access to your photo library. This permission is only used for the avatar feature.

5. Data Storage

Some app data is stored locally on your device. If you sign in, certain data such as progress and daily records may also be synced to cloud services associated with your account.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not directly collect your full payment card information. Subscription status and purchase entitlements are handled through Apple’s StoreKit framework.

7. Your Choices

  • You may choose whether to create an account.
  • You may choose whether to upload an avatar.
  • You may request deletion of your account and synced cloud data from within the app.
  • Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Apple subscription. Subscription management must be handled through your Apple account.
